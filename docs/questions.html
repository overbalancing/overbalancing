0005-SEESAW-BEHAVIOR — answered

Owner answer, 2026-10-19: fixed stacks. Balls do not roll; weight-triggered launches, edge wrapping and opponent-first split-screen routing are recorded in the game concept.

0006-FLIGHT-RULE — answered

For arbitrary stacks and arriving weights, what determines which balls launch and how far: the arriving ball or side totals, and the top opposing ball or multiple balls? What exactly is one field? How does a landing trigger the next launch?

Owner answer, 2026-10-19: use the sum of the entire stack; launch only the top ball; one field means the neighboring stack position. The exact launch trigger and general distance formula remain unresolved in the game concept, particularly the different outcomes of two separate weight-1 arrivals and one weight-2 arrival.

0007-FLIGHT-TRIGGER — answered

Does launch distance use the arriving ball weight minus the opposing stack sum, even when the landing side already holds balls, or the difference of both full stack sums with a separate launch condition? Owner answer, 2026-10-19: compare the complete stack totals and trigger a launch when the seesaw flips fully from one heavy side to the opposite heavy side without balancing in between. Example: left total 3, right total 2, arriving weight 2 on the right gives 3 versus 4; the top left ball flies one field right, from the resulting difference 1.

0008-JOKER-COLORS — answered

Owner answer, 2026-10-20: 5% of generated balls have two or three colors. Level 1 has no jokers. A level-2 joker can only use the two available colors; higher levels can supply two or three unique colors.

0009-SUPPLY-AND-WEIGHTS — answered

Owner answer, 2026-10-20: show the next two balls above each stack and keep one ball in the chute, replaced immediately after each drop. Integer weights in the specified mathematical interval L/2 through L use ceil(L/2), at least 1; source selection is clarified separately in 0010.
