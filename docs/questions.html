0005-SEESAW-BEHAVIOR — answered

Owner answer, 2026-10-19: fixed stacks. Balls do not roll; weight-triggered launches, edge wrapping and opponent-first split-screen routing are recorded in the game concept.

0006-FLIGHT-RULE — answered

For arbitrary stacks and arriving weights, what determines which balls launch and how far: the arriving ball or side totals, and the top opposing ball or multiple balls? What exactly is one field? How does a landing trigger the next launch?

Owner answer, 2026-10-19: use the sum of the entire stack; launch only the top ball; one field means the neighboring stack position. The full-reversal trigger and complete-stack difference were subsequently confirmed in 0007-FLIGHT-TRIGGER.

0007-FLIGHT-TRIGGER — answered

Does launch distance use the arriving ball weight minus the opposing stack sum, even when the landing side already holds balls, or the difference of both full stack sums with a separate launch condition? Owner answer, 2026-10-19: compare the complete stack totals and trigger a launch when the seesaw flips fully from one heavy side to the opposite heavy side without balancing in between. Example: left total 3, right total 2, arriving weight 2 on the right gives 3 versus 4; the top left ball flies one field right, from the resulting difference 1.

0008-JOKER-COLORS — answered

Owner answer, 2026-10-20: 5% of generated balls have two or three colors. Level 1 has no jokers. A level-2 joker can only use the two available colors; higher levels can supply two or three unique colors.

0011-STACK-OVERFLOW — answered

Owner answer, 2026-10-20: exceeding the available 9/8/7 stack positions is Game Over. This also applies when tilting reduces the available height of an already occupied stack.

0012-COMPRESSION-MASS — answered

Owner answer, 2026-10-20: compression preserves the summed weight of all five balls. Five weight-one balls become one weight-five ball.

0013-JOKER-MATCHING — answered

Owner answer, 2026-10-20: jokers bridge color changes. A red group can spread through a red/blue joker into blue balls and through another joker into further colors. Each removed ball scores once.

0014-RESOLUTION-ORDER — answered

Owner correction, 2026-10-20: evaluate the seesaw before clearing. When a ball lands on a balanced seesaw and lowers its side, matching uses the resulting final physical position. Compression preserves weight; evaluate weight and tilt before detecting horizontal matches, and repeat after gravity and landings. This supersedes the earlier clear-before-tilt answer. The owner additionally requests a short clearing animation that permits a quickly following ball to join and be removed with the active connected group.

0016-SPEZIAL-GEWICHTE — answered

0017-BONUS-MUSTER — answered

Does a row laser require at least five matching balls in a horizontal run? Does a column laser require three adjacent stacks of at least four balls each, cleared together?

Owner answer, 2026-10-20: Yes: the row laser rewards at least five horizontally adjacent matching balls. The column laser rewards three adjacent stacks of at least four balls, cleared together.

0018-GOLDSTERNE — answered

Owner answer, 2026-10-20: Confirmed: consume the gold stars, permanently enlarge the board by one row in every lane, at most three times; award the achievement on the third expansion. Five gold stars require ten level-earned silver stars.

0019-SUPERHERZ — answered

When a superheart crosses an edge, do the eight bombs or stones land directly in the eight stacks and immediately resolve contact effects, or enter supply?

Owner answer, 2026-10-20: The chosen eight bombs or stones land directly on the eight stacks, rather than entering supply.

0020-BOMBEN-AUSLOESER — answered

Does a bomb detonate on contact with any nonzero-weight ball, or only another bomb? A bomb resting on an empty stack would wait for a later contact.

Owner answer, 2026-10-20: A bomb detonates on direct contact above or below with any ball whose weight is greater than zero. A bomb resting on an empty stack remains inert.

0021-SUPERHERZ-AUFLOESEN — answered

Do superhearts remain until thrown over an edge, or do three horizontally adjacent superhearts clear as well? Red hearts are treated as their own kind, not connected to ordinary red balls.

Owner answer: Three horizontally adjacent superhearts clear like hearts.
