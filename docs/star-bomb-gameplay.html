Overbalancing documentation · Feature proposal

Stars, bombs and stones

This first special-ball feature adds silver and gold stars, bombs and stones to the existing puzzle. Primitive icons distinguish the kinds without external assets or new dependencies. Hearts, superhearts, lasers, feathers and black holes belong to later features and are not enabled here.

Recognize the kinds

  • Silver and gold stars are colorless, weigh zero and use a star silhouette with a readable zero.
  • Bombs weigh ten and use a dark body with a fuse, distinct from ordinary colored balls.
  • Stones weigh five and use an irregular gray rock silhouette. They neither compress nor match.

Keep the same kind icon and weight on previews, held balls, stationary balls and flights. The normal palette keeps the owner-approved plain early colors and subtle later marks.

Star rewards

Completing a level inserts a silver star at the front of the triggering lane's supply. The held ball remains unchanged and displaced previews are retained. Silver-on-silver vertical contact makes one gold star, including contact after a flight.

Three horizontal silver stars clear the board. That clear increases displayed score without advancing the level. Three or more horizontal gold stars are consumed to add one permanent row to every lane for the current game. At most three bonus rows can be earned; the third unlocks the achievement. Bonus rows survive level changes, while a new game resets them.

Show a compact bonus-row counter, preserving the level/score/progress header and sixteen supply previews. At the maximum expansion, heavy, balanced and light sides have twelve, eleven and ten rows. The viewport must accommodate the expanded board without hiding weights, totals or the held ball.

Bomb contacts and blasts

Three percent of generated balls are bombs. Direct contact immediately above or below a positive-weight ball detonates a bomb; either participant may arrive. A zero-weight star alone does not trigger it. Each blast affects its center and neighboring horizontal, vertical and diagonal cells. Reached bombs chain; other specials destroyed by the blast disappear without activating their effects.

Blast score follows removed weight times one hundred, counting each destroyed ball once. Stones can be destroyed by blasts or other special effects. A brief expanding primitive ring can communicate a blast; detailed counts and outcomes belong in logs.

Crossing the edge

A launched silver or gold star stays a star until its route crosses the board boundary, then becomes a weight-ten bomb once. It continues along the already computed route as that bomb. The visual change must occur at the boundary, not at launch or on every animation frame.

Verification status

On 2026-10-20, the UX engineer inspected actual 390-pixel renders of the special catalog, silver merge, bomb effect and settled outcome, silver board clear, third gold expansion and paired star edge-crossing frames, plus the three-row desktop view. Visual review passes at these sizes: silver/gold silhouettes, bomb fuse and rock shape are distinct; weights 0, 10 and 5 are readable; the expanded board, held chute, sixteen previews and eight numeric totals remain visible without overlap. The compact header shows Rows +3 and the third-expansion notice without crowding the previews. Paired boundary frames show silver zero before crossing and bomb ten afterward.

The lead separately reports actual fixture outcomes: the bomb chain destroys four balls for score 2600 at level two with a silver reward at the supply front; the silver clear shows score 1000 at level one with the next-level requirement unchanged at 1000; silver contact produces gold zero; the gold fixture reaches three bonus rows and the achievement. Reset checks clear fixture state. Still images establish displayed states rather than every transition. Physical phone execution and customer acceptance remain pending.

Try the fixtures

cargo run --locked -p overbalancing-app --bin overbalancing -- --special-demo

Press Down for silver merging. Other focused fixtures are --special-bomb-demo, --special-silver-demo, --special-gold-demo, --special-bonus-demo and --special-edge-demo. Backspace resets the game.

Selected-stack warning

On 2026-10-23, the UX engineer ran the warning fixture under Vulkan/Xvfb and inspected six successive frames at 390 × 844 pixels. The selected stack total alternates between its normal color and orange while its displayed value remains zero; no numeric prediction replaces the real total. Moving the chute one position right stops the original stack warning. No ball was dropped during this check. This verifies the warning presentation on Linux, not physical-phone interaction.
