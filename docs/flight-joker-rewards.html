Flight-earned ultimate jokers and queued rewards

Status: implementation for review. Weight, award frequency and supply order use explicit lead assumptions while owner questions 0023–0025 remain unanswered. Progress on implementation does not mean those choices have been approved.

Recognize the ultimate joker

A rainbow rosette distinguishes this special from ordinary two- or three-color joker sectors. Under the current working interpretation it weighs zero, matches every ordinary color and another ultimate joker, and never compresses. It does not match colorless stars, bombs, stones or utilities. Use the same procedural rosette and readable dark zero in previews, the chute, stacks and motion; no external asset or new dependency is needed.

A qualifying horizontal run still needs a common ordinary color, or three ultimate jokers. Red, red, ultimate can qualify; red, ultimate, blue alone does not. Once a valid group exists, an ultimate joker can bridge connected ordinary color groups.

Earn rewards through flights

The provisional policy awards one ultimate joker per committed ordinary clear phase caused by a launched ball, including inherited gravity-clear phases. Multiple components in one phase do not multiply the reward. Direct chute clears, blast damage, laser damage and silver board clears do not earn it by themselves.

The original clear cause and triggering lane stay attached to the phase. A flight merely joining an already qualified direct-clear group does not convert it into a flight-earned reward; a direct ball joining a flight-created group does not erase that eligibility.

Preserve the next scheduled ball

The provisional supply order preserves held A and the next scheduled B, then delivers earned rewards in their original order before old C: A, B, S1, S2, C. Rewards from further events append to the batch. No held ball, visible preview or deferred tail is discarded. Moving the chute neither consumes rewards nor changes their earning lane. This replaces the earlier immediate-front reward policy for this feature.

Reset clears pending rewards, causal state and transit. Ordinary score and level rules remain unchanged by delivery order.

Verification status

On 2026-10-23, the UX engineer ran both focused fixtures under Vulkan/Xvfb with actual X11 Down and Backspace inputs, capturing 390 × 844 and 1100 × 1000 views. Visual review passes: the rainbow rosette is distinct from ordinary sector balls, its central zero is readable on the board and while dropping, and previews, totals and controls do not overlap. Reset returns the game to an empty board.

See 0023, 0024 and 0025 for the outstanding owner decisions.

Five rapid Down presses in the reward-order fixture produced logged kind/weight/color order: normal red two, normal blue three, silver zero, ultimate zero, normal yellow two. This verifies A, B, S1, S2, C through actual consumption. In the flight fixture, the launched landing cleared four balls for 400 points and queued exactly one ultimate joker together with a feather in the causal lane, after its reserved next preview. The retained board rosette remains visible afterward. These fixtures do not replace rule tests for causality exclusions, rollback or hidden-tail preservation. Physical phone execution and customer acceptance remain separate; questions 0023–0025 are still provisional.
