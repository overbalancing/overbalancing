Overbalancing documentation · Feature proposal

Row lasers, column lasers and feathers

Utility balls are colorless, weigh zero and use procedural icons. They do not match or compress. Row and column lasers have horizontal and vertical arrow cues; a feather has its own curved silhouette. No external asset or new dependency is needed.

Earn and activate utilities

An ordinary clear containing a maximal matching horizontal run of at least five earns a row laser. Clearing four consecutive balls in each of at least three adjacent stacks in one ordinary clear earns a column laser. A nonempty ordinary clear earns a feather when every affected seesaw was balanced immediately before removal.

Rewards enter the front of the original triggering lane's supply without replacing the held ball or losing previews. Blast damage, silver clears and laser damage do not create ordinary-clear rewards.

A utility activates on direct vertical contact above or below a positive-weight ball, whether it arrives or was already resting in the stack. Flight contact also counts. Empty lanes and all-zero stacks leave the utility inert; a later positive-weight arrival can activate it.

Effects and scoring

  • A row laser removes its own physical row across the board, accounting for tilt and bonus rows.
  • A column laser removes its entire lane.
  • A feather sets the remaining balls in its lane to weight zero without changing their kinds, colors or order.

A utility is consumed once when activated. Laser removal adds displayed points using removed weight times one hundred but does not advance levels. Feather weight changes award no removal points. Destroying a utility through an area effect does not itself activate it; actual contacts caused by the resulting gravity still follow the normal contact rules.

A feathered bomb remains a bomb and can detonate on later positive-weight contact. A feathered stone remains a nonmatching, noncompressing stone. Zero-weight normal balls retain their color matching but award zero weight points.

Read and control the scene

Short white horizontal or vertical feedback identifies a laser's affected area; a pale feather wave identifies the changed lane. Keep icons, weights, sixteen previews and eight stack totals readable at 390-pixel portrait width. Effects supplement the changed board and do not require new buttons or diagnostic text.

Down drops the held ball; Left/Right or pointer/touch selects the chute position. Backspace resets the board, pending clears, rewards, effects and active transit.

Verification status

On 2026-10-23, the UX engineer ran all six focused fixtures under Vulkan/Xvfb with actual X11 Down and Backspace inputs. Before, effect, settled, desktop and reset captures were inspected. Visual review passes at 390 × 844 and 1100 × 1000: horizontal/vertical utility icons, feather silhouette and numeric zero are distinguishable; directional white beams and the feather lane tint are visible; sixteen previews and eight totals remain readable without overlap.

The row fixture displays 500 points and the column fixture 700, both remaining at level one with 1000 points needed. Feathered stone and normal balls retain their distinct shapes/colors while showing zero and awarding no points. An isolated feather remains inert after its first drop; a later positive-weight ball consumes it and becomes weight zero, confirmed by the activation log. Row and feather rewards appear at the front of lane six's previews after a 600-point clear. The column-reward fixture shows 1700 points at level two with silver and column-laser rewards in lane four. These are one-based visible lane labels. Reset returns the fixtures to empty boards. Physical phone execution and customer acceptance remain separate.
