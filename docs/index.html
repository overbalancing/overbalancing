Current scope

The foundation and initial view are owner-accepted. The placement sandbox adds configurable balls, fixed stacks, total weights and illustrative seesaw tilt. Launches and full game resolution rules remain future work.

Build checks and a displayed scene do not establish runtime compatibility on every device. Platform runtime evidence is tracked in the first-view change.
