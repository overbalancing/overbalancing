Overpalancing documentation · Level supply

Flight reactions

The level-supply view presents accepted drops, wrapped launches and chained landings with original primitive animation. The core determines game outcomes; animation presents them without changing distances, weights, colors or the order of reactions. No external art assets or new dependency are needed.

Interaction while a reaction is active

Repeated Down, Space or Enter presses can accept successive drops immediately, including while other drops, flights or clearing windows are active. Four quick presses must not be reduced to one accepted drop by a busy guard. Each accepted drop refills the held ball immediately from the current destination supply. Dropping and held balls remain visually distinct. Game over or an explicit error stops further accepted drops. Diagnostics belong in logs.

Backspace resets the board. The visible Drop and Reset buttons have been removed. Reset cancels the active reaction and clears its visual effects as well as restoring game state. Inspection may remain available without starting a second chain. A completed atomic landing can reveal a persistent GAME OVER state; Backspace remains the reset command.

Read the flight

Show only the moving ball's numeric weight inside the ball, with its colors represented by fills. Retain readable weight labels and all four seesaws at a 390-pixel portrait viewport. Detailed reaction status belongs in diagnostic logs.

Record the full launch distance in diagnostic logs. To keep presentation bounded for enormous wrapped distances, an animation may show at most one visible lap plus the remainder. This is a visual abbreviation only: the actual launch distance, wrapped destination and reaction outcome remain unchanged. Do not label the abbreviated arc as the complete traveled distance.

Try a reproducible reaction

cargo run --locked -p overpalancing-app --bin overpalancing -- --flight-demo

Press Down, Space or Enter to start the fixture's drop and reaction chain. Backspace cancels the reaction and restores a normal empty level-five board rather than reseeding the fixture. For ordinary play with weights that can demonstrate reactions, launch with --level 3. Normal level one contains only weight-one balls and does not demonstrate the full flip reaction by itself.
