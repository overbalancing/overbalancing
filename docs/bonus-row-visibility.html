Overbalancing documentation

Show every earned bonus row

The renderer previously displayed at most nine balls per stack even when the game retained additional balls within earned capacity. This fix changes presentation, not the stored board or capacity rules.

With three bonus rows, lowered, balanced and raised sides can contain twelve, eleven and ten balls. Every valid row must be visible at once in the normal board view. Inspection limits use the expanded visible range, and stale offsets are clamped when the required range shrinks or the game resets.

Verification status

On 2026-10-24, the UX engineer ran the focused fixture under Vulkan/Xvfb with actual X11 wheel and Backspace inputs. Visual review passes at 390 × 844 and 1100 × 1000: the lowered stack shows all twelve balls, both balanced stacks show eleven each, and the raised stack shows ten. Every top ball, numeric weight, sixteen supply previews and eight totals remains visible without clipping or overlap. The three-digit weight 100 and totals 36, 35, 35 and 30 remain readable. Eight wheel-up steps do not shift or hide valid rows. Backspace restores an empty board with zero bonus rows and normal framing. This is actual Linux presentation proof, not physical phone execution or customer acceptance.
