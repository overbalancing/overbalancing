Overbalancing documentation · Flight reactions

Puzzle resolution and progression

This feature extends level supply and flight reactions with compression, connected color clearing, score and automatic level progression. It uses the existing primitive graphics and controls; no external asset or new dependency is required.

Resolution order

Resolve compression, evaluate clearing using the final tilted platform geometry, award clearing points, then continue seesaw reactions and flights. Only five vertically consecutive, identical single-color balls compress into one ball of that same color. Its weight is the sum of the five weights. Two- and three-color joker balls never compress and break a compression run, even if they contain the surrounding balls' color. Compression alone awards no clearing points.

A qualifying horizontal run starts connected clearing through orthogonally adjacent matching colors. A multicolor joker can change color membership as the connection crosses it, joining differently colored portions of the cleared group. Count each removed ball once for score even when more than one route reaches it. Joker matching is a color-change connection, not an extra copy of the ball.

Clearing window

After compression, the final tilted geometry determines the initial qualifying clear group. The marked group remains pending for a fixed 450 milliseconds with a visible glow. Its already qualified balls stay marked even if a later arrival changes the tilt; matching connected neighbors can join it using the current geometry. A quick follow-up ball may therefore join the pending group. The original group is not canceled or unqualified by that arrival. Actual removal and score happen once, at commitment, with each removed ball counted once. Reset cancels the pending window and its effects.

Score and level

The initial level is one. The next-level requirements are 1000 points for 1 to 2 and 2000 for 2 to 3. From level 3 onward, each step requires an additional 2000 × L points: 6000 for 3 to 4, 8000 for 4 to 5, and so on. Surplus carries into the next level's progress. Display the current level, accumulated score and points still needed so the player can distinguish lifetime score from the current target.

Level changes affect newly generated balls only. Already held balls and visible previews keep their colors and weights. A level-up notice must not suggest those existing balls have changed. The command-line --level 3 option remains useful for comparing higher-level supply and reactions.

Feedback and controls

Keep all sixteen preview balls and the held chute ball visible at 390-pixel portrait width. Use compact score and progress lines rather than shrinking essential text. The resulting ball shows its numeric weight. Compression counts, removed-ball counts and awarded-point diagnostics are recorded in logs. Balls no longer display color-ID or level text.

Use the established chute selection controls; Down, Space or Enter drops the held ball. Further drops are accepted immediately during other drops, flights and clearing windows, until game over or an explicit error. Four quick Down presses must remain four independent accepted actions. An arriving matching ball can join a pending clearing group before commitment. Backspace resets. The visible Drop/Reset buttons, balance-status rows and Target/Held/Weight summary are removed. Eight plain numeric stack totals remain below their respective stacks, without L/R prefixes. Vertical falling animation is three times faster than before this cleanup.

Full-reversal warning

The selected chute position previews the actual landing resolver on cloned state. A full reversal blinks the affected stack’s existing numeric total without changing that number. Preview does not mutate the board, score, pending clears, supply or random stream. It must account for special effects rather than merely comparing a hypothetical added weight.

Try the combined fixture

cargo run --locked -p overbalancing-app --bin overbalancing -- --puzzle-demo

Press Space once. The fixture compresses one group, clears six connected balls through the joker bridge and awards 600 points; the remaining yellow ball has weight six. The inspected landscape feedback reads 5->1 x1 | Cleared 6 | +600. Feedback is temporary, so a later settled screenshot may show only the resulting board and persistent score. Ordinary play still starts at level one; --level 3 starts a higher-level comparison.
