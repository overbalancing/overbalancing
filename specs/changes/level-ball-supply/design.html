Level supply design

Keep ball identities, palette membership, supply consumption and weight generation deterministic in core with injected random samples. Rendering and entropy seeding belong in app. Palette IDs grow with level; no arbitrary three-color level cap. Individual balls still carry at most three colors unless the owner changes that rule.

Keep source supply and destination stack separate so chute redirection consumes exactly the chosen source. No automatic source ordering, level progression, timing, score, failure cap or flight rule may be invented.

Questions 0008/0009 confirm two/three-color jokers (none on level 1), two previews per stack, one held chute ball and 9/8/7 vertical positions. Questions 0010/0011 preserve the remaining refill-source and overflow rules. The old sandbox guides remain historical; the new default view removes their manual configuration controls.
