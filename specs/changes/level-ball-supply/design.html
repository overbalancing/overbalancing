Level supply design

Keep ball identities, palette membership, supply consumption and weight generation deterministic in core with injected random samples. Rendering and entropy seeding belong in app. Palette IDs grow with level; no arbitrary three-color level cap. Individual balls still carry at most three colors unless the owner changes that rule.

Keep source supply and destination stack separate so chute redirection consumes exactly the chosen source. Source is the current destination lane: shift its two previews and generate one replacement. Exceeding the current 9/8/7 positions is Game Over and stops further drops until reset. Do not invent level progression, scoring or flight resolution in this feature.

Questions 0008/0009 confirm two/three-color jokers (none on level 1), two previews per stack, one held chute ball and 9/8/7 vertical positions. Questions 0010/0011 confirm destination-lane refill and Game Over on overflow. The old sandbox guides remain historical; the new default view removes their manual configuration controls.
