Level supply acceptance

  • Level 1 uses exactly one available color; level 4 uses four; colors are not capped at the old three preset colors.
  • Generated weights stay inside the confirmed inclusive level range and are never zero.
  • Joker frequency is controlled by a named code configuration initialized to 5%; deterministic tests verify threshold boundaries.
  • Each of eight upper supplies remains visible above its stack. Chute destination may differ from the consumed source.
  • Placing a ball preserves its generated weight and colors and refreshes only the consumed supply.
  • No Lower/Higher/Weight/Color buttons remain in the default view. All controls fit portrait 390px and landscape.
  • No screenshot art is copied and no unconfirmed flight or joker matching rule is introduced.
