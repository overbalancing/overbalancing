Playable stars, bombs and stones

Status: implementation in progress; platform proof and owner acceptance pending.

Add a complete first special-ball feature to the existing puzzle: silver/gold stars, bombs and stones with recognizable visuals, working contacts and edge conversions, level rewards and permanent capacity expansion within the game. Owner answers 0016–0020 establish these mechanics.

  • Silver/gold stars are colorless and weight zero. Each completed level supplies a silver star; silver-on-silver contact, including flight contact, creates gold.
  • Three horizontal silver stars clear the board with displayed score but no level advancement. Three or more horizontal gold stars are consumed for one bonus row in every lane, up to three rows; the third unlocks an achievement.
  • Three percent of generated balls are bombs of weight 10. Contact above or below any positive-weight ball detonates a bomb; radius-one physical Chebyshev blasts chain other bombs and destroy other specials without activating them.
  • Stones weigh 5, do not compress or match, and can be removed only by blasts or other special effects.
  • A launched star crossing an edge becomes a bomb once, while normal wrapped flights retain existing behavior.

The broader special-ball proposal and capability retain later hearts, lasers, feathers and black holes. Those kinds are not partially enabled in this feature. No new dependency or asset purchase is required.

This first package also applies the owner’s later-level pacing correction (1000, 2000, then 2000 times the current level) and a tilt-launch warning based on a cloned real resolver. The warning blinks an existing numeric stack total without replacing its value.
