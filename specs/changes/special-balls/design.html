Special balls design proposal

This is an architecture proposal, not a choice of unresolved gameplay values.

Concrete domain data

Extend ball data with a concrete BallKind discriminant for ordinary, silver star, gold star, bomb, heart, superheart, stone, row laser, column laser and feather. Retain checked integer weights and explicit color membership. A later implementation must distinguish colored matching kinds from nonmatching kinds; do not invent colors for stars or stones. Preserve independent joker metadata: ordinary multicolor balls still do not compress.

Do not use a zero weight as the sole kind tag. Zero is meaningful after a feather, and only designated utility kinds have nonzero-contact activation rules. Existing positive-weight placement validation must become kind-aware without opening unrestricted zero-weight ordinary generation.

Score and progression

Represent a resolved award with separate displayed-score and level-eligible-point fields. Normal eligible clears may advance both, hearts contribute neither, and silver-star whole-board clears and row/column laser effects must award displayed score without level advancement. Apply both counters atomically with checked arithmetic. Points for bomb damage, stones, other destroyed specials and mixed effects remain unresolved and must not be guessed.

Deterministic effects

Process concrete events for landing contact, merges, clear commits, blast damage, utility contact, edge crossing and generated rewards. Inject entropy into random events; render timing must not change sampling order. Bomb chains use a deterministic queue and a visited identity set so each affected bomb explodes once. Damage unions cells before mutation. Special destruction suppresses activation except for the explicit bomb-chain rule.

Resolve a bomb's radius against physical board positions, including diagonals, rather than confusing stack indices on differently tilted seesaws. Snapshot identities and remap through removals or use stable event-local identities; never clear an unrelated ball after vector collapse. The exact two-bomb contact centers and ordering must be settled before coding the blast transaction.

Owned flights and edge transformations

Each moving ball remains owned by exactly one in-transit record. Apply an edge conversion once for the appropriate crossing event. Stars become bombs; hearts choose bomb or stone equally; superhearts choose the all-lane bomb or stone outcome equally. Do not silently alter flight distance or sample a new random outcome every render frame. Placement of all-lane results and black-hole precedence remain unresolved.

Supply and utility contact

Separate ordinary random generation, per-level silver-star delivery and event-generated rewards. Preserve the existing seventeen visible/held balls except where the confirmed reward-insertion policy explicitly replaces or inserts one. A reward is delivered immediately to the triggering lane's supply; the exact front/back behavior needs clarification.

A utility resting on an empty or all-zero stack remains inert. Test activation from a nonzero landing below or above it as required by the confirmed contact rule, including flight landings. Model activation separately from blast destruction so destroying a utility does not accidentally fire it. Feather effects target one whole stack; the target choice must be explicit.

Board modifiers and delivery

Keep bonus capacity and black-hole lifetime in plain board/session data, not in a BallKind or a rendering component. Derive visible row counts from base capacities plus the confirmed bonus rule. Lifetime counting uses accepted chute throws if confirmed; automatic flights and animation frames must not silently decrement it.

No new crate or physics engine is proposed. Keep the deterministic resolver in the core and presentation/input in the app. Every feature boundary includes focused domain tests, real visual review and preserved ownership/error/reset behavior.
