Special balls design proposal

Owner answers 0016–0020 establish the initial stars/bombs/stones implementation boundary; later effects remain staged.

Concrete domain data

Extend ball data with a concrete BallKind discriminant initially for ordinary, silver star, gold star, bomb and stone. Later deliveries add heart, superheart, row laser, column laser and feather. Retain checked integer weights and explicit color membership. BallColors may be empty only for colorless special kinds. Normal balls remain positive and colored. Silver/gold stars weigh zero, bombs 10 and stones 5, with kind-aware constructors and validation. Preserve independent joker metadata: ordinary multicolor balls still do not compress.

Do not use a zero weight as the sole kind tag. Zero is meaningful after a feather, and only designated utility kinds have nonzero-contact activation rules. Existing positive-weight placement validation must become kind-aware without opening unrestricted zero-weight ordinary generation.

Score and progression

Represent a resolved award with separate displayed-score and level-eligible-point fields. Normal eligible clears may advance both, hearts contribute neither, and silver-star whole-board clears and row/column laser effects must award displayed score without level advancement. Apply both counters atomically with checked arithmetic. Bomb damage retains removed weight times 100: normal, bomb and stone weights contribute while stars weigh zero. Silver board clears award displayed score only for the entire removal and suppress destroyed-bomb activation.

Deterministic effects

Process concrete events for landing contact, merges, clear commits, blast damage, utility contact, edge crossing and generated rewards. Inject entropy into random events; render timing must not change sampling order. Bomb chains use a deterministic queue and a visited identity set so each affected bomb explodes once. Damage unions cells before mutation. Special destruction suppresses activation except for the explicit bomb-chain rule.

Resolve a bomb's radius against physical board positions, including diagonals, rather than confusing stack indices on differently tilted seesaws. Snapshot identities and remap through removals or use stable event-local identities; never clear an unrelated ball after vector collapse. A bomb reacts to any positive-weight vertical neighbor, whether the bomb or neighbor arrives above or below it. Zero-weight stars alone do not trigger contact. Snapshot both bomb positions for bomb-on-bomb contact and deduplicate the resulting damage.

Owned flights and edge transformations

Each moving ball remains owned by exactly one in-transit record. Apply an edge conversion once for the appropriate crossing event. Stars become bombs; hearts choose bomb or stone equally; superhearts choose the all-lane bomb or stone outcome equally. Do not silently alter flight distance or sample a new random outcome every render frame. The confirmed superheart result is eight direct drops, one in each lane. Black-hole precedence remains a later effect-specific detail.

Supply and utility contact

Separate ordinary random generation, per-level silver-star delivery and event-generated rewards. Preserve the existing seventeen visible/held balls except where the confirmed reward-insertion policy explicitly replaces or inserts one. A reward is delivered immediately to the triggering lane's supply; insert it at the front and retain the held ball and all existing previews in order, using hidden overflow for displaced previews. Consume preserved backlog before exposing a fresh replacement; newly generated balls may queue behind it without being discarded.

A utility resting on an empty or all-zero stack remains inert. Test activation from a nonzero landing below or above it as required by the confirmed contact rule, including flight landings. Model activation separately from blast destruction so destroying a utility does not accidentally fire it. Feather effects target one whole stack; the target choice must be explicit.

Board modifiers and delivery

Keep bonus capacity and black-hole lifetime in plain board/session data, not in a BallKind or a rendering component. Derive visible row counts from base capacities plus zero to three earned bonus rows, persistent across levels within the game. The third expansion emits an achievement event. Physical offsets must use the expanded maximum (nine plus bonus rows minus capacity) to prevent underflow and unintended alignment changes. Black-hole lifetime counting remains a later effect-specific decision; automatic flights and animation frames must not silently decrement it.

No new crate or physics engine is proposed. Keep the deterministic resolver in the core and presentation/input in the app. Every feature boundary includes focused domain tests, real visual review and preserved ownership/error/reset behavior.

Core and app contract

Outcome events expose merges, blast damage, score-only board clears, expansions, achievements and rewards. Pending phases retain the initial triggering lane so rewards go to its supply even after follow-up arrivals. The app visualizes events without duplicating domain rules.

A star crossing an edge becomes a weight-10 bomb exactly once while retaining the already determined flight route. Rightward crossing is distance greater than seven minus origin; leftward crossing is distance greater than origin. This detects full wraps and avoids adding a u64 distance to the origin. A wrapped destination alone cannot determine whether an edge was crossed.
