Extend the existing level-driven puzzle with silver/gold stars, bombs, stones, hearts/superhearts, zero-weight utility rewards and temporary black holes. The complete owner intent is recorded in the capability specification.

  2. Hearts and superhearts, matching eligibility, merge rules and all-lane edge outcomes.
  3. Row/column laser and feather rewards, immediate supply delivery and nonzero-contact activation.

Keep at most four pending PRs, all targeting main with dependencies and review order stated. Existing pending work counts toward that limit.

Use dedicated feature branches for the special-ball batches, based on the required accepted or explicitly stacked prerequisites. Keep each review branch within one to six focused commits.

First usable feature

Deliver Normal, SilverStar, GoldStar, Bomb and Stone kinds: colorless stars weigh zero, bombs weigh 10 and stones weigh 5. Include three-percent random bombs, positive-weight vertical contact detonation, physical blast chains, inactive destruction of other specials, per-level silver rewards queued at the front without losing old supply, silver-to-gold contact, silver score-only board clear, gold row expansion up to three and the third-expansion achievement. Include star-to-bomb edge conversion and complete visible feedback and tests.

Gold rows are consumed and expand every lane across subsequent levels of the current game. Bomb damage carries forward existing removed-weight-times-100 scoring. Later hearts, utilities and black holes remain specified but are not partially enabled here. Confirmed answers and remaining effect details are tracked in the question register.
