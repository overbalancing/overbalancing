Observable acceptance scenarios

Scenario Expected observation
Launch A single game view shows exactly four seesaws with pivots, at least one ball and a visible dispenser.
Ball readability Every displayed ball has a readable weight and a cue identifying its color beyond color alone.
Desktop interaction On-screen instructions identify keyboard and pointer controls; each documented selection/drop action produces visible feedback.
Touch interaction A tap on the documented control produces the same demo action without requiring hover or a physical keyboard.
Resize and orientation At desktop resize and mobile portrait/landscape sizes, all four seesaws and required controls remain visible and usable without overlap that hides controls.
Scope honesty The demo does not claim compression, matching, scoring or final physics are implemented.
