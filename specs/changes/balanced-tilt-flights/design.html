Overbalancing documentation

Balanced-launch and gold-threshold design

Both the direct Board::land API and the puzzle resolver recognize balanced-to-heavy transitions in addition to opposite-heavy reversals. Left-heavy results launch from the right stack leftward; right-heavy results launch from the left stack rightward. Pop at most the top ball. If the rising stack is empty, emit no flight.

Evaluate distance from complete totals before the pop. Keep modulo arithmetic, wrapped-route conversion and explicit in-transit ownership. Becoming balanced, remaining balanced and retaining the same heavy side do not launch. Removal alone does not recursively launch another ball. Existing resolver phase boundaries and clearing/compression priority remain authoritative.

The warning predictor runs landing preparation on cloned live state, so actual emitted flights govern both balanced tilts and full reversals without mutating play state.

Three-gold expansion

The resolver threshold is three gold stars in a maximal horizontal run. Two stars do not expand. Three do, and longer contiguous runs still earn one expansion rather than overlapping rewards. Disjoint qualifying rows can each earn an expansion up to the existing total of three. Consume gold even when that cap is already reached; do not repeat the third-expansion achievement. App fixtures must use this same threshold.

Acceptance scenarios

  • With equal positive totals, an arrival on either side emits exactly the opposing top ball in the correct direction at the final weight difference.
  • Balanced zero-total stacks can launch an existing weightless special; an empty rising stack emits nothing.
  • Reaching balance, unchanged balance and adding to an already heavy side emit nothing.
  • Board plus owned ordinary flights conserve count and weight; explicit star-to-bomb edge conversion retains its separately defined weight change. Wrapping remains correct.
  • Puzzle resolution and selected-lane warning agree, including clear protection and cloned-state immutability.
  • Two versus three gold stars distinguish the new expansion boundary; the three-row cap, run consumption and third-expansion achievement stay intact.
