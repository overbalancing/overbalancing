Placement design

The core owns plain deterministic balls, eight ordered stacks, total weights, balance direction and reset. The Bevy app owns controls, animation and a bounded illustrative tilt. Fixed stacks do not roll.

Weights 1–3 and red/blue/yellow are selectable sandbox presets, not final game limits. Explicit placement avoids inventing dispenser sequencing. Growing stacks must remain inspectable without inventing a loss condition.

Keyboard, mouse and touch must support every operation. Touch controls must not cause duplicate placement. Readable labels accompany colors. Existing render-smoke flags remain available.
