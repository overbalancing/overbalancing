Overbalancing documentation

Placement acceptance scenarios

  • Select any of eight stack positions; placing adds exactly one configured ball to that position and preserves order.
  • Left weight 1 and right weight 1 balance; another right weight 1 lowers the right side without launching a ball in this sandbox.
  • Changing selected ball properties does not change balls already placed.
  • Every side total reflects its entire stack; reset clears all stacks.
  • Keyboard and touch can configure, select, place and reset. Tall stacks remain inspectable.
  • The interface clearly identifies the sandbox and pending flight/matching rules.
