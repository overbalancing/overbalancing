Row lasers, column lasers and feathers

Status: implementation and local verification complete; cross-platform CI and owner acceptance pending. The stars/bombs/stones prerequisite is merged.

Reward qualifying ordinary clears with usable zero-weight utility balls. A horizontal clear run of at least five earns a row laser. Three adjacent removed vertical runs each at least four balls high earn a column laser. If every affected seesaw is balanced before the clear, earn a feather.

Deliver each reward immediately to the front of the triggering lane's supply without losing its held ball, previews or queued backlog. A utility resting against only zero-weight balls or on an empty stack remains inert. Direct positive-weight contact above or below activates it, including a later arriving or flying ball.

A row laser removes its entire physical row. A column laser removes its entire lane. A feather sets the weights of all remaining balls in its own lane to zero while retaining their kinds and identities. Laser score does not advance the level.

This is a complete gameplay feature with reward delivery, visuals, activation feedback, tests and reset behavior. Hearts and black holes remain separate; no new dependency or physics engine is proposed. See the special-ball capability.
