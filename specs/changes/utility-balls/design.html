Overbalancing documentation

Utility ball design

Kind and runtime weight

Add RowLaser, ColumnLaser and Feather as concrete colorless, zero-weight kinds. They do not participate in normal color matching or compression. Keep generation constructors for normal balls, bombs and stones at their established positive baseline weights. Runtime validation must nevertheless allow a feathered Normal, Bomb or Stone to retain its kind and palette with weight zero; do not change kind or discard its identity to represent the effect.

A zero-weight bomb still has bomb behavior and can later detonate when a positive-weight neighbor contacts it. A zero-weight stone remains unmatchable and uncompressible. Zero-weight ordinary balls keep their color matching behavior but contribute zero points.

Reward detection

At an ordinary clear commit, snapshot selected cell identities, physical rows and all affected seesaw balances before removing anything. Do not derive rewards from a blast's deferred points, a silver-star board clear, laser damage or an empty selected set.

  • Each maximal horizontal selected run of at least five qualifies for a row-laser reward. Deduplicate overlapping color interpretations of the same physical run.
  • A maximal adjacent group of at least three lanes qualifies for a column-laser reward when each contains a removed vertical run of at least four consecutive stack positions. Inspect removed runs, not merely total stack height or cells cleared in unrelated events.
  • A nonempty ordinary clear qualifies for a feather only if every seesaw containing any selected cell was balanced in the pre-clear snapshot.

Use the clear phase's original trigger lane for all resulting supply rewards; joined follow-up arrivals do not move that provenance. Existing Reward events and lossless front-queued supply storage can carry these kinds. Rewards are not automatically landed during their creation, so a reward cannot recursively activate itself.

Contact and targets

A utility activates only on direct vertical contact above or below a positive-weight ball. Check both an arriving utility and a utility already resting in the lane when a later ball lands. Flight landings follow the same rule. An empty lane and an all-zero stack cannot trigger activation.

Consume an activating utility exactly once. A row laser targets the physical row of its own cell across all lanes, accounting for current tilt and bonus capacity. A column laser targets its own entire lane. A feather sets every remaining ball in its own lane to zero weight, preserving kinds, palettes and order.

Capture contact causes and area targets before mutation. Keep a deterministic work queue and remap identities through removals. Area destruction is not a synthetic landing or direct-contact event: removing a special does not itself dispatch that special's activation. Actual new contacts created by gravity are evaluated through ordinary contact rules on the resulting board. The bomb-specific explicit chain rule remains separate.

Score, phases and ownership

Laser damage uses checked removed weight times 100, counted once for overlapping targets, and awards displayed score only. It cannot advance levels or generate ordinary-clear rewards. Feather changes weight without awarding removal points. Keep displayed and level-eligible points separate in pending transactions.

Preserve the existing contact/tilt/match order, fixed clear windows, sticky normal match membership, all in-transit ownership and lossless supply. Snapshot target geometry before an effect, then resolve resulting contacts, weight changes and matches through deterministic core phases. Every queued utility activates at most once; reward generation adds supply entries rather than synchronous board landings, so no arbitrary iteration cap is needed.

The app renders distinct utility symbols, inert versus activated state and row/column/weight-change feedback. It does not recalculate reward geometry or special effects. Reset clears board effects, supply rewards, pending clears and active/queued transit.

Focused validation

Test horizontal 4 versus 5 and overlapping joker runs; two versus three adjacent four-ball removed runs; nonadjacent or incomplete removed columns; balanced versus one tilted affected seesaw; no feather for an empty clear. Test resting utilities and arrivals above/below, all-zero nonactivation, flight contact, physical-row targets with tilt/bonus rows, zeroed bomb/stone identity, score-only lasers, deduplicated damage and full transaction rollback.
