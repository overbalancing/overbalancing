Overpalancing documentation

Flight acceptance scenarios

Confirmed full reversal

Left total 3 and right total 2 receive weight 2 on the right. Final totals 3 and 4 launch only the top left ball one field right. A transition to or from balanced does not launch; adding to an already heavier side does not launch.

Explicit two-flight chain

Prepare lane 0 with bottom weight 1 and top weight 2, lane 1 with weight 2, lane 4 with weight 1 and lane 5 with weight 2. Land weight 5 in lane 1. The top lane-0 ball flies four fields right to lane 4. Its explicit landing reverses that seesaw and launches lane 5's top weight 2 one field left to lane 4. Its next landing settles. Final totals are lane 0 = 1, lane 1 = 7 and lane 4 = 5. All other lanes are empty.

At every transition, board weight plus in-transit weight equals 13, and board count plus in-transit count equals six. A flight is owned by the caller until landed; source supply is not consumed for a chain reaction.

Large arithmetic and wrap

Side totals may exceed u32 and must be compared as u64. With positive u32 arrivals, a strict reversal's distance is arrival weight minus the prior heavy-side lead. The lead is at least one, so the maximum possible single-arrival distance is u32::MAX minus one. The public distance remains u64 for arithmetic robustness and possible future weight changes. Tests cover this maximum valid distance, both wrap directions and distances divisible by eight.

Application acceptance

Animate the initial drop and each emitted flight in sequence. Disable additional player drops while a ball is in transit. A chain landing uses the same core landing rule and consumes no additional supply. Check game over after the landing reaction has removed its launched top ball. Reset cancels in-transit state. Matching, compression and multiplayer routing remain outside this change.
