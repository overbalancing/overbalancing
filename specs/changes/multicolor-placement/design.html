Multicolor design

Represent nonempty subsets of red/blue/yellow in a concrete core value. Preserve weight independently. Ball construction and color toggles cannot produce an empty set or more than three colors. Existing placed balls retain their own configuration.

Color controls toggle membership; at least one color stays selected. Keyboard R/B/Y and pointer/touch use the same operation. Selected colors are visible in controls and text. Render single fills, two half sectors or three sectors with numeric weight on a separate label line. Preserve readable390px portrait layout and row inspection.
