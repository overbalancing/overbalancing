Overpalancing documentation

Extended palette cue design

Threshold and finite mapping

A named DISTINCT_BASE_COLORS setting initially equals six. IDs zero through five receive no symbol or pattern. Above that threshold, select a filled triangle, square, diamond, plus, X or star with a repeating stripe flag using bounded integer operations on the identity. The threshold is easy to tune; the vocabulary is finite and repeats. No palette allocation or iteration proportional to the level is allowed.

Same-hue presentation

Fill glyphs with a darker tone derived from the sector color, initially multiplying its color channels by 0.78, without changing its underlying hue. Keep diagonal bands subtle, initially blending toward white by eighteen percent. Do not use the superseded black outlined shapes, black ball rims or white weight pill.

Render the weight directly with contrasting ink chosen for the underlying ball appearance. Do not introduce color-ID labels or a numeric legend. Symbols and patterns occupy their own sector and leave the weight readable.

Shared geometry

The ball_visual build/spawn path serves stacks, previews, the held ball and all moving drops or flights. The shared radius and row-height geometry remain consistent across platforms, panning, landing coordinates, historical launch positions and targeting rings. Filled joker glyphs stay clipped to their own sector and circle; base-color sectors remain plain.

Transforms provide movement and clear pulses without changing cue identity. No external asset, dependency or core type is added. Arbitrary ColorId values, including u32::MAX, require only bounded work for at most three visible sectors.

Evidence

Tests cover plain base colors, higher-ID symbols, deterministic repeating mappings, bounded clipping and identical static/moving construction. Real render review covers desktop and 390-pixel portrait, plain and marked colors, two- and three-color jokers and motion. Geometry tests cannot establish perceptual legibility; owner and UX review use the actual rendered result.
