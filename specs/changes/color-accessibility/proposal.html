Procedural symbols and patterns for ball colors

Status: implemented and locally verified; platform CI and owner acceptance pending.

Similar-looking hues make colors difficult to recognize. Add deterministic geometric symbols and patterns to balls and joker sectors while keeping independent weight labels and preserving the owner’s choice to remove in-ball color-ID text. Use one shared visual builder for stacks, supply and moving balls so cues remain consistent during play.

No artwork request, external asset, crate or core-rule change is needed. The cue vocabulary is finite; combinations may repeat at higher color IDs, without claiming a unique visible encoding for every identity.

Capability: Color accessibility. This presentation change builds on the existing puzzle-resolution behavior.
