Procedural cues for extended palette colors

Status: implementation and local verification in progress; owner acceptance pending.

Keep the first six colors plain, then add filled symbols and subtle same-hue patterns to higher color IDs. This preserves the owner's preferred simple base palette while making expanded palettes easier to recognize. Keep only the independent weight as ball text, drawn directly without a white pill.

Use one shared builder for stacks, supply and transit. Remove black rims and outlined symbols. The finite symbol/pattern vocabulary eventually repeats; no globally unique or certified recognition guarantee is claimed.

Capability: Color accessibility. No artwork request, new crate or core-rule change is needed. The earlier all-colors and outlined-symbol design is superseded by this owner-selected appearance.
