Flight-earned ultimate jokers and delayed FIFO rewards

Status: implementation and local verification complete for review using explicit lead assumptions. Platform CI and owner feature acceptance remain pending. Owner decisions 0023–0025 remain pending; continued implementation is not an answered question.

Reward ordinary clears caused by launched-ball landings with an ultimate joker that matches every ordinary color. Preserve the next already scheduled ball and deliver earned specials consecutively in FIFO order without discarding the held ball, previews or hidden supply backlog.

Provisional working choices

  • 0023: the ultimate joker weighs zero.
  • 0024: each committed flight-caused ordinary clear phase, including inherited gravity-clear phases, awards one ultimate joker, rather than one per connected component.
  • 0025: held A and previews B, C followed by earned S1 and S2 deliver A, B, S1, S2, C. Keep A and B scheduled; preserve C and every later ball behind the reward batch.

These choices are lead-selected working interpretations, not owner-approved rules. The owner may revise them through 0023, 0024 and 0025.

Direct chute clears do not earn this reward. The ultimate joker matches ordinary balls and ordinary jokers, never colorless stars, bombs, stones or utilities, and never participates in compression. No new crate or random probability is introduced for earning it.
