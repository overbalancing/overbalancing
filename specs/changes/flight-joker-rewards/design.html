Universal ordinary matching

Add a concrete UltimateJoker kind with weight zero under the current working interpretation. It has no fabricated ColorId and does not require allocating every color in the level. Treat it as matching every real ordinary color and another ultimate joker, but not other colorless special kinds. It may bridge ordinary color components after an ordinary horizontal seed qualifies.

A run of three ultimate jokers also qualifies directly without inventing a palette identity. Red, red, ultimate qualifies as a red seed; red, ultimate, blue alone does not share one color across all three and therefore does not create an initial seed. Existing shared-color cascades may nevertheless cross an ultimate joker into other ordinary colors. The ultimate joker never compresses, including when surrounded by five otherwise equal-colored balls.

Immutable clear-phase cause

Distinguish direct chute landings from launched-ball landings at the deterministic core boundary. When a flight first creates an ordinary pending clear, record flight causality together with the triggering lane in the pending phase. Preserve that cause through the clear animation, commitment, gravity and any resulting secondary ordinary clear phases. Earlier deferred bomb or laser points do not own a later first ordinary clear: record the new ordinary phase’s actual landing lane and cause.

A flight that joins or expands an already qualified chute-caused phase does not retroactively become its cause. An unrelated flight arriving during that window cannot earn a reward for the old group. A direct throw joining a flight-created group does not erase its original cause. Once the causal chain settles, a new independent direct clear starts without flight eligibility. A later emitted ball landing is a flight event even if an earlier direct throw originally launched it.

The current working policy awards one ultimate joker for each nonempty ordinary matched clear commit carrying this flight cause, including inherited gravity phases. Disconnected components in that commit do not multiply the award. Bomb damage, laser damage and silver board clears alone do not earn it. Ordinary scoring and level eligibility remain unchanged by reward delivery.

Delayed FIFO delivery

Use the existing reward event's immutable causal lane. Enqueue rewards immediately but preserve the held ball and the first scheduled preview before the reward batch. The explicit provisional fixture is A, B, S1, S2, C for held A, previews B/C and rewards S1 then S2. Further rewards append to the same lane's pending batch in event order, rather than being inserted in reverse order or interleaved with old preview tails.

Preserve all displaced preview and generated balls in the deferred tail. Compact repeated reward counts avoid allocation proportional to a large level gain. Moving the chute neither consumes rewards nor changes the lane that earned them. Reset clears pending rewards, previews, causal state and owned transit together. This sequence replaces the previous immediate-front delivery policy for the new feature while leaving historical merged behavior documented as such.

App and resolver boundaries

The app identifies a real flight landing from its owned transit path and calls the corresponding core preparation entry point. It does not infer flight causality from the current chute position, elapsed animation time, a score increase or the mere presence of other queued flights. Pending state and rewards remain part of transactional clone/commit handling.

Use the common ball builder for a recognizable ultimate-joker symbol across supply, held, stacked and flying balls. Preserve actual weights, fixed clear deadlines, sticky matching membership and the existing dissolution-before-compression rule. Match and reward preview must clone actual pending causal context and must not issue live rewards, consume entropy or mutate the supply.
