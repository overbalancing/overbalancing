Color accessibility

Status: capability specification. Delivery and acceptance are tracked in the active change.

Owner-selected appearance

The first six palette colors, IDs zero through five, remain plain colored balls. A named, tunable threshold initially set to six controls where procedural cues begin. Higher color IDs use filled geometric symbols and subtle patterns derived from the ball's own hue, rather than black outlines or strong white overlays.

  • Use the same visual builder for placed balls, held and upcoming supply balls, drops and launched balls. A color identity retains its appearance as it moves or the level increases.
  • Two- and three-color jokers show the corresponding cue only within each qualifying higher-ID sector. Plain base-color sectors remain unmarked.
  • Use filled triangle, square, diamond, plus, X and star families with a repeating stripe flag. Higher IDs use a finite vocabulary; no infinitely unique visual encoding is claimed.
  • Do not draw a black outer rim, outlined symbol or white weight pill. Render the independent weight directly with contrasting ink. Do not reintroduce numeric color IDs inside balls or add a numeric legend.
  • Keep symbols, hue-toned patterns and weights legible at desktop and narrow portrait sizes. Clear glow, moving-ball targets and game-over overlays must remain compatible.
  • Generate bounded geometry per visible ball or sector, independent of the current level. Do not allocate every available palette color. Treat u32::MAX as a valid identity input.

Limits and verification

Only colors beyond the initial six receive symbols; the owner deliberately retains plain base colors. Finite higher-ID combinations repeat. Internal IDs and diagnostic logs preserve exact identity. This change does not alter matching, joker, compression, weight, flight or scoring rules and is not a color-vision accessibility certification.

Verify the base-color threshold, deterministic cue selection, shared static/moving construction, sector clipping and large IDs. Inspect actual desktop and 390-pixel portrait renders containing plain colors, higher-ID cues, both joker sizes and moving balls. Owner acceptance remains separate from tests and geometric correctness.
