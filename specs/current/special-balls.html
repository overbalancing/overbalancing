Overpalancing documentation

Special balls and board effects

Status: owner-requested capability, not yet implemented. Detailed mechanics with unresolved values must remain explicit until clarified. See the delivery proposal and question register.

Stars and capacity rewards

  • Provide a silver star after each completed level.
  • A silver star landing on a silver star creates a gold star. Contact through a flight also counts.
  • Three silver stars in a horizontal row clear the board and award score, but that score must not advance the level.
  • A horizontal row of at least five gold stars awards a bonus row. At most three bonus rows are available, with an associated achievement.
  • A star crossing a board edge becomes a bomb.

The spawn placement of the level star, star weights and colors, gold conversion result, exact bonus-row effect and achievement trigger require clarification. Do not treat an ordinary color match as a special-star activation without its own rule.

Bombs and stones

  • Random generation includes bombs at three percent probability. A bomb weighs 10.
  • A bomb landing on a bomb detonates both. Each explosion affects the radius-one Chebyshev neighborhood: its center and adjacent horizontal, vertical and diagonal positions.
  • Blasts trigger other bombs as a chain reaction.
  • Other special balls destroyed by a blast disappear without activating their special effects.
  • A stone weighs 5, never compresses and never matches. Only an explosion or a special effect can destroy a stone.

Damage scoring, the bomb contact centers and precise interaction ordering with other effects remain part of the explicit implementation decision; do not silently grant points or trigger destroyed specials.

Hearts

  • Random generation includes hearts at two percent probability. Hearts participate in ordinary matching but award no points.
  • A heart crossing a board edge becomes a bomb or a stone with equal probability.
  • Five hearts become one superheart.
  • Five superhearts become one superheart.
  • A superheart crossing an edge produces bombs in all lanes or stones in all lanes, with equal probability.

Heart and superheart weights, their colors, the color eligibility of vertical five-heart groups, resulting weights, and placement/activation of the all-lane outcome must be clarified. These rules are independent of the existing prohibition on compressing ordinary multicolor jokers.

Utility rewards

  • A qualifying clear of at least five balls rewards a row laser. Its bright white horizontal beam removes the entire physical row at the height where it is placed. The exact qualifying pattern is pending question 0017.
  • The owner also requested an analogous whole-column reward for “3 4er Stapelauflösen”; the intended qualifying pattern is pending question 0017.
  • Row and column laser effects award points without advancing the level.
  • If all affected seesaws are balanced at the clear, award a feather. A feather changes the weight of every ball in one stack to zero.
  • Utility balls weigh zero and activate only when in contact with a nonzero-weight ball. An empty stack or a stack made entirely of zero-weight balls does not activate them.
  • Activation can occur on landing or through a nonzero ball on top, including flight landings.
  • A reward enters the supply of the triggering lane immediately.

Clarify reward insertion within the existing two-preview supply, which stack or row a utility targets, how the column reward is counted, and whether the balance test uses the pre-clear or resulting board. The contact rule does not permit an ordinary generated zero-weight ball.

Black holes

A cleared O-shaped group of at least three by three positions whose center does not clear creates black holes outside the board edges. The chute cannot select a black hole. Balls launched into them are swallowed for thirty chute throws.

The exact ring geometry and event boundary, lifetime counting, renewal behavior and precedence over ordinary wrap conversions need explicit treatment. Black holes are board effects, not silently selectable extra stack lanes.

Cross-cutting constraints

Preserve deterministic core behavior, owned in-transit balls, visible clear phases, checked arithmetic and transactional error handling. Separate displayed score from level-eligible points so silver-star clears can award the former without the latter. Random outcomes consume injected entropy in the core, with documented event ordering. Do not add a physics engine or external dependency for these mechanics.
